FAQ

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok links work end-to-end today. X/Twitter detection is on, with download support rolling out as we harden it against that platform's frequent page changes.

Is OmniSaver free to use?

Yes. Paste a link, pick a format, and download — no account, subscription or payment required.

Do you store the videos I download?

No. Every download is streamed straight from the source to your device as it converts; nothing is saved on our servers before, during or after.

Can I download audio only, as MP3?

Yes. Once a link is fetched, choose the MP3 option under "Audio only" to get just the sound track instead of the full video.

What video quality can I download?

OmniSaver lists every resolution the source actually publishes for that video, from the highest available down to lower, smaller-file-size options — pick whichever fits your device or data plan.

Can I download WhatsApp videos this way?

No — WhatsApp doesn't expose a public link for shared media the way the platforms above do, so there's nothing for a URL-based tool to fetch. Forwarding or saving from within WhatsApp itself is the only option there.

Is this legal?

Only download content you own, have permission to use, or that's covered by the platform's own license terms (e.g. an explicit "save" feature). You're responsible for complying with each platform's terms of service and applicable copyright law.
